Overview

Video Extractor AI is a modern AI-powered SaaS platform that converts long YouTube videos, podcasts, influencer content, and educational videos into structured actionable knowledge.

Users can:

Download videos/audio
Extract transcripts
Remove filler content
Generate AI summaries
Create step-by-step notes
Chat with video content
Export structured literature

The platform is optimized for:

Influencer videos
Podcasts
Business content
Educational videos
Self-improvement videos
Finance videos
Tech tutorials
